# 2025 surveillance audit — finding history

- Audit dates: 17 to 21 November 2025
- Audit scope: the whole information security management system
- Findings: 3 nonconformities (1 major · 2 minor) and 2 observations

The findings themselves are in `previous-findings_2025.csv`. This note gives only
the background you need to read that table.

## What the grades mean

| Grade | Meaning | Action due |
|---|---|---|
| Major | The control was found not to operate | Set by the auditor — for this audit 2025-12-31, about six weeks after the audit closed |
| Minor | The control exists but evidence of carrying it out falls short | End of the next quarter |
| Observation | Not a nonconformity, but an improvement was recommended | End of the next half-year |

## Action and verification

Once action is done, the evidence of the action is kept, and the audit owner checks
that evidence to complete verification. The closed date and the verified date are
different things. If action is done but not yet verified, `Verification` reads
`Not verified`.

An empty `Evidence` cell means no action evidence has been received yet. It may
mean no action was taken, or that action was taken and no evidence was handed in.
The table alone cannot tell the two apart.

## Using this material

The control numbers in the findings use the same scheme as the Annex A control
numbers. Match them against this year evidence and you can see where each control
raised last year stands now.

But if a control was closed and its evidence has fallen short again, you cannot
settle that it is a recurrence. Circumstances may have changed after the action, or
the finding and this year evidence may be about different things. The auditor rules.
