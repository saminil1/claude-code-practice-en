# 02. Access Control Guideline

**Purpose** — Sets how access rights to information systems and data are granted, changed, and withdrawn, and how the people who hold them are authenticated.

## Article 1 (Scope)

This guideline applies to the accounts and rights on every information system the company runs or uses. An outside service is covered too where it holds company information.

## Article 2 (Granting rights)

Rights are granted only to the least extent the role actually needs, and wide rights are never granted in advance for the sake of convenience. Granting runs through a request from the department head and an approval by the information security team, and neither step is skipped for urgency.

The request records the person, the system, the rights, the reason, and the period. A right with an end date is withdrawn automatically when that date passes.

## Article 3 (Managing accounts)

One account belongs to one person and shared accounts are not created. Where equipment leaves no choice, use is logged and the password changed afterwards.

An account used only between systems is registered with its purpose and owner, and interactive login is blocked. An unused account is disabled without delay.

## Article 4 (Reviewing rights)

**Whether granted access rights are still appropriate is reviewed twice a year.** Each department head reviews their own people and submits the result.

Rights found to be unnecessary are withdrawn at once, and the withdrawal is recorded with its reason.

## Article 5 (Joiners, movers, and leavers)

Rights are granted on the day a person joins and changed on the day they move, and rights that no longer fit the new role are withdrawn at the same time.

**A leaver's account is disabled on the day the person leaves.** It is kept disabled rather than deleted for the period the record rules require.

## Article 6 (Authentication)

A password meets the rules the company sets and is not reused across services. Where a system holds personal data, a second factor is added.

## Article 7 (Records)

Access records are kept for the period the law and the contracts require and protected against alteration. Who may read them is limited in advance.

## Article 8 (Action on a breach)

Using someone else's account, or reaching beyond the rights granted, is a breach and it is handled under Article 9 of the information security policy.

---

This guideline takes effect on 1 January 2026.
