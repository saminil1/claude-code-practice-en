# 03. Asset Management Guideline

**Purpose** — Sets how information assets are identified, recorded, owned, classified, and finally disposed of when they are no longer needed.

## Article 1 (Scope)

This guideline covers hardware, software, data, documents, and services that hold company information, whether the company owns them or rents them.

## Article 2 (The asset list)

Every asset is entered in the asset list with its name, type, owner, location, and classification, and an asset that is not on the list is not treated as protected. The list is the single place the company looks to answer what it holds.

**The asset list is refreshed once a quarter.** Each department reports what it holds and the information security team consolidates those reports into the single list, settling any disagreement with the owner before it is published.

## Article 3 (Owners)

Every asset has exactly one owner. The owner decides who may reach the asset, approves changes to it, and answers for the way it is protected, and ownership passes with the role rather than with the person.

## Article 4 (Classification)

Information is classified as public, internal, confidential, or restricted. The class is decided by the owner and marked on the document or the file.

Handling, storage, and transfer follow the rules set for the class. Where a document mixes classes, the highest class applies to the whole.

## Article 5 (Movement and return)

Taking an asset out of the workplace needs the owner's approval and the fact of it is recorded. Equipment is returned on the day the person leaves or moves to another role, and the return is confirmed by the department head.

## Article 6 (Disposal)

A storage medium is disposed of so that the data cannot be recovered, and the disposal is recorded. A medium that held personal data is destroyed physically.

## Article 7 (Checks)

The information security team samples the list against what is actually held and reports any gap to the owners, so that it can be put right before the next quarterly refresh.

---

This guideline takes effect on 1 January 2026.
