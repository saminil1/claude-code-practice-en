# 04. Personnel Security Guideline

**Purpose** — Sets the security duties that attach to a person before joining, while working, and at the point of leaving the company.

## Article 1 (Before joining)

What a role requires is confirmed before an offer is made, and where that role handles personal data or system privileges, the checks the law allows are carried out first and the result is recorded.

## Article 2 (Terms of engagement)

A confidentiality undertaking is signed on joining and stays in force after leaving. The undertaking names what may not be disclosed and for how long it holds, in words plain enough that the person can follow it.

The same undertaking is signed by contract staff, seconded staff, and interns before any access is given, and a copy of it is kept with the personnel record.

## Article 3 (While working)

A person uses company information only for company work. Taking it out for a private purpose is not allowed, whatever the medium.

A workstation is locked whenever it is left, and printed material is not left anywhere others can read it. Material no longer needed is disposed of rather than stacked.

## Article 4 (Moving roles)

On moving to another role, rights that no longer fit are withdrawn and the material held is handed over, and what was handed over is recorded so nothing is left sitting in a personal folder.

## Article 5 (Leaving)

On leaving, equipment, documents, and identity items are returned and information held on personal devices is deleted, and the department head confirms in writing.

## Article 6 (Discipline)

A breach of the security rules is handled under the staff rules, and the standard applied is the same for all, whatever the position held is.

---

This guideline takes effect on 1 January 2026.
Amendment history: first issue, no amendments to date.
