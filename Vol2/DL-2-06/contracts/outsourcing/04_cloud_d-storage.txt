──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Art. 1 (Purpose) This contract sets out what is needed when the controller
        outsources personal information processing work to the processor.

Art. 3 (Purpose and scope of the outsourced work)
        The processor shall process personal information within the scope of data storage and
        backup.

Art. 5 (Limits on sub-outsourcing)
        The processor may sub-outsource only after giving the controller prior notice and
        obtaining approval.

Art. 7 (Security measures for personal information)
        The processor shall encrypt stored data, apply access control, and verify backup
        integrity.

Art. 9 (Management and supervision of the processor)
        The controller may review periodically the personal information protection measures of
        the processor.

Art. 12 (Liability including damages)
        In the event of a data leak the processor shall notify without delay and compensate for
        the damage.

Art. 15 (Term and handling at termination)
        At the end of the contract the processor shall delete the stored data by a method that
        cannot be recovered.
