──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Art. 1 (Purpose) This contract sets out what is needed when the controller
        outsources personal information processing work to the processor.

Art. 3 (Purpose and scope of the outsourced work)
        The processor shall not process the personal information entrusted to it for any
        purpose other than delivery and the collection of returns.

Art. 5 (Limits on sub-outsourcing)
        The processor shall not sub-outsource to a third party without the prior written
        consent of the controller.

Art. 6 (Security measures for personal information)
        The processor shall apply access rights management, retention of access logs for
        6 months, and encryption in transit.

Art. 8 (Management and supervision of the processor)
        The controller shall inspect the processor's handling of personal information at
        least once a year.

Art. 10 (Liability including damages)
        Where a data subject suffers damage through the fault of the processor, the processor
        shall be liable for compensation.

Art. 12 (Term and handling at termination)
        At the end of the contract the processor shall destroy the personal information within
        30 days and submit a certificate of destruction.
