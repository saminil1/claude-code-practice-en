Art. 1 (Purpose) This contract sets out what is needed when the controller
        outsources personal information processing work to the processor.

Art. 2 (Purpose and scope of the outsourced work)
        The processor shall process personal information only within the scope of customer
        service and order taking.

Art. 6 (Limits on sub-outsourcing)
        The processor shall in no case sub-outsource without the written approval of the
        controller.

Art. 7 (Security measures for personal information)
        The processor shall encrypt call recordings, separate agent rights, and control removable
        media.

Art. 9 (Management and supervision of the processor)
        The controller shall carry out an on-site inspection once every half-year, and the
        processor shall cooperate.

Art. 11 (Liability including damages)
        Where a leak arises from the intent or negligence of the processor, the processor shall
        compensate for the damage.

Art. 14 (Term and handling at termination)
        Within 15 days of the end of the contract the processor shall destroy the personal
        information and report the result.
