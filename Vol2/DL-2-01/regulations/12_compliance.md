# 12. Compliance Guideline

**Purpose** — Sets how the company keeps to the laws, the contracts, and its own rules, and how compliance with them is checked.

## Article 1 (The legal register)

**The register of laws and contract terms that bear on information security is refreshed once a year.** Each entry names the clause and the department that answers for keeping to it, and a change in the law is entered when it is published.

## Article 2 (Internal audit)

**An internal audit of the security management system is carried out once a year.** The auditor never audits work that they themselves did, and where the company is too small for that, an outside reviewer is used.

A finding is recorded together with its grounds, and the correction is then tracked through to completion rather than closed on a promise alone.

## Article 3 (Records)

Records are kept for the period that the law and the contracts require, protected against loss and alteration, and then disposed of once that period ends rather than held indefinitely.

## Article 4 (Personal data)

Personal data is collected, used, and destroyed within the bounds that the law allows, and the lawful basis for each use is recorded before that use begins.

Where personal data leaves the company, the recipient and the ground for sending it are both recorded before it goes out.

## Article 5 (Intellectual property)

Software and content are used within the licence that was granted, and the licences held are recorded and then checked against whatever is actually installed on company equipment.

## Article 6 (Independent review)

Where an outside review or a certification audit is carried out, its findings are handled the same way as the internal ones.

---

This guideline takes effect on 1 January 2026.
