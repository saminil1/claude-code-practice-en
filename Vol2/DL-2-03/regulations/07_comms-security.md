# 07. Communications Security Guideline

**Purpose** — Sets how the network is protected, and how information is transferred safely inside the company and beyond it.

## Article 1 (Network design)

The network is divided into segments according to the sensitivity of what each one holds, and traffic between segments passes through a control point. The design is kept as a diagram and updated whenever it changes.

## Article 2 (Access from outside)

Reaching the internal network from outside is only through an approved route, with authentication and encryption both in place, and that route and the people allowed on it are registered and reviewed.

## Article 3 (Wireless)

A wireless network is encrypted and separated by its purpose, and a guest network is held apart from the internal network with no route running between the two. The key for a guest network is changed on a schedule.

## Article 4 (Transfer)

Confidential information sent outside is encrypted and the recipient is confirmed before it goes. Where a large file passes through an outside service, only an approved service is used for that purpose.

A file is not attached to a message where the same file can be shared through a controlled route instead, because an attachment leaves the company's reach the moment it is sent.

## Article 5 (Electronic mail)

Mail is not used to carry restricted information, and a message carrying a link or an attachment from an unknown sender is reported to the information security team rather than opened. A message that has already been opened is still reported.

## Article 6 (Outside services)

An outside service that will hold company information is assessed before it is adopted, and the assessment is recorded together with the decision and with the person who made it. The assessment is repeated when the service changes what it holds.

## Article 7 (Monitoring)

Traffic at the boundary is monitored, and a pattern that departs from the ordinary is examined and passed on where it matters, rather than dismissed as noise because it is inconvenient.

## Article 8 (Rules on equipment)

Adding equipment to the network needs registration and approval in advance. Equipment found unregistered on the network is disconnected and then its owner is traced.

---

This guideline takes effect on 1 January 2026.
Amendment history: first issue, no amendments to date.
