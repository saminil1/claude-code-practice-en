# 05. Physical Security Guideline

**Purpose** — Sets how the areas that hold information assets are protected, and how entry to each of those areas is controlled and recorded.

## Article 1 (Zones)

The workplace is divided into a public zone, a work zone, and a controlled zone, and the controlled zone covers the server room and the document store.

## Article 2 (Entry control)

Entry to the work zone is by identity card. Entry to the controlled zone needs approval in advance and is recorded with the time of entry and of exit, so that presence can be reconstructed afterwards.

A visitor is registered at reception, wears a visitor card while inside, and is accompanied throughout the visit by the person who invited them.

## Article 3 (Equipment)

Server and network equipment sits inside the controlled zone, and power, temperature, and humidity are held in the range the equipment needs. A failure in any of the three raises an alarm rather than being found later.

Cabling is laid so that it cannot easily be tapped or damaged, and the layout of it is kept as a record that is updated when the layout changes.

## Article 4 (Media and documents)

Confidential documents are held in a locked cabinet and portable media in a place with entry control, and printed output is collected from the printer at once rather than left sitting in the tray.

## Article 5 (Working away)

Equipment taken outside is never left unattended and the screen is not used where others can read it, and any loss is reported on the same day that it is noticed.

## Article 6 (Maintenance)

An outside engineer working in the controlled zone is accompanied throughout, and the work is recorded with what was done and by whom it was done.

---

This guideline takes effect on 1 January 2026.
