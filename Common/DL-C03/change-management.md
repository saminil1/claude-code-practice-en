# C-5. The seven steps of change management (Vol.2 Ch.12)

The timing rules — never Friday afternoon · never just before a holiday · one at a time · leave time to undo it.

**[Table C-3] The seven steps of change management**

| Step | The key question |
|---|---|
| ① Request | What are we changing and why |
| ② Impact analysis | What does it touch · what could we not confirm |
| ③ Approval | Did somebody with the authority decide |
| ④ Plan | When · who · how · the way back |
| ⑤ Execute | Did it go to plan |
| ⑥ Confirm | Did it do what we meant · is everything else fine |
| ⑦ Record | What changed, when and why |
