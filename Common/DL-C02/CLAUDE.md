# CLAUDE.md

## Organization
- company · size · sector
- certifications held and their renewal dates

## Where the files are
- read only: 
- writable: 

## Working rules
- Read files one at a time and report the count per file
- Cite grounds in the form [doc number-clause]
- Leave a cell blank if you do not know, and say why
- Keep "could not confirm" apart from "not there"

## Prohibitions (highest priority)
- Never modify (read-only folder). Results go to (write folder) only.
- Never put personal data in an output. Mask it and report separately.
- Never rule conformant or not, grade a risk or set a priority.
  Give the grounds and the points to review.
- Approval required for what you cannot undo: delete, send, transfer.

## On conflict
- Where rules conflict, the prohibitions always win.

## Style
- Reports: plain past tense, conclusion first
- Raising a point: never assert — write "this needs confirming"
